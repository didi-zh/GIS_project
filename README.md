# GIS Data Project (MON Competition)

This repository contains a Geographic Information System (GIS) project developed using **ArcGIS** for a competition organised by the Ministry of Education and Science (MON).

---

### My Role & Contributions
* **Regional Research (Africa):** Collected, verified, and structured ecological and spatial data regarding grazing pressures, desertification, and habitat fragmentation across critical African ecosystems (including the Sahel, East African Savannas, and the Congo Basin).
* **Storytelling & Content Design:** Developed the narrative structure, textual analyses, and thematic flow of the interactive web presentation to effectively communicate complex spatial and environmental relationships.

## About the Project

**The Relationship Between Livestock Farming and Biodiversity** is an interactive web project and geographic analysis developed with **ArcGIS Experience Builder** and **ArcGIS StoryMaps**. The project explores the complex connection between grazing practices, livestock production, and ecosystem health, contrasting global critical zones of ecological pressure with sustainable models where pastoral farming actively supports biodiversity.

### Project Objective
To illustrate through interactive maps, spatial data, and regional analyses how intensive livestock farming impacts landscapes and wildlife habitats worldwide, while demonstrating how responsible grazing and conservation partnerships can preserve endangered species and restore ecological balance.

### Geographic Scope

The project covers key critical zones and case studies across five major regions:

* **United States:** The Great Plains, Kentucky, Pennsylvania, Idaho, Washington, and Arizona (analysis of ranching, dairy farming, and the environmental footprint of feed crop production).
* **South America:** Gran Chaco, Southern Amazonia, Cerrado, Santa Cruz (Bolivia), and Colombian Amazonia (deforestation and pasture expansion).
* **Asia:** Western Ghats, Northeast India, the Himalayas and foothills, Inner Mongolia, Tibetan Plateau, Yangtze River Basin, and the Indo-Gangetic Plain (soil degradation, overgrazing, and pressure on water basins).
* **Africa:** The Sahel, East African Savannas, Miombo Woodlands, Congo Basin, Madagascar, Guinean Forests of West Africa, and South African Grasslands (desertification, habitat fragmentation, and obstruction of wildlife migration corridors).
* **Europe and Bulgaria (Case Study):** A comparative analysis between the European **Natura 2000** network and the **Emerald Network**, alongside an examination of sustainable organic grazing and vulture conservation in the **Madzharovo** region (Eastern Rhodopes).


## Data Sources and Study Partners

### Global Livestock and Deforestation Data
* **Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)** – Statistical databases (*FAOSTAT*) and the *Global Livestock Environmental Assessment Model (GLEAM)* regarding land use, grazing pressure, and feed crop expansion.
* **Global Forest Watch (GFW)** – Satellite monitoring data on deforestation frontiers and forest loss in critical regions such as Gran Chaco, the Amazon Basin, and the Congo Basin.
* **World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF)** – Research and reports (*Living Planet Report*) on biodiversity hotspots and agricultural land-use impacts.

### European and Regional Protected Networks
* **European Environment Agency (EEA)** – Official spatial layers and ecological assessment data for the **Natura 2000** network.
* **Council of Europe** – Documentation and spatial boundaries for the **Emerald Network** across non-EU European territories.
* **Ministry of Environment and Water of Bulgaria (MOEW)** – National environmental reports and protected area datasets.

### Bulgarian Case Study and Sustainable Practices (Madzharovo / Eastern Rhodopes)
* **Bulgarian Society for the Protection of Birds (BSPB)** – Field data and conservation models regarding the protection of Griffon and Egyptian vultures in the Eastern Rhodopes and the role of extensive livestock grazing in maintaining open mosaic habitats.
* **Rewilding Rhodopes Foundation** – Initiatives and research on wildlife restoration, natural grazing systems, and biodiversity conservation in the Madzharovo region.
* **Harmonica** – Best practices in organic dairy farming, sustainable pasture management, and biodiversity-friendly agriculture in Bulgaria.


## Tools & Technologies
- **ArcGIS (Online / Experience Builder / StoryMaps)** – For GIS analysis, spatial data collection, interactive web interface development, and map creation.
- **Microsoft Excel / Python** – For spatial data processing and coordinate conversion (DD to DMS).
